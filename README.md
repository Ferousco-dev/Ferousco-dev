## 📁 Featured Projects

### 📚 [Orastudy](https://github.com/Ferousco-dev/Orastudy)
A campus-focused PDF and study material hub. Students can find, browse, and pull course materials in one place instead of scattered group chats and drives, making past questions and lecture notes easy to access.

### 🔨 [Forge](https://github.com/Ferousco-dev/Forge)
A tool built for informal workers, giving them a simple, accessible way to manage their work and finances. Designed around dignity and ease of use for people often overlooked by mainstream apps.

### 💳 [TapPay](https://github.com/Ferousco-dev/TapPay)
An NFC-based payments app. Tap-to-pay made simple, letting users send and receive money with a single contactless tap.
